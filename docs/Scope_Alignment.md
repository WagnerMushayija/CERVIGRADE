## Scope changes, in short

The detector replaced the sliding-window design because the supervisor asked for it and it gave a usable photo-level result. Mendeley changed role because its labels are per photo, not per cell. PostgreSQL, React, separate services and hosting were replaced by a single local backend, plain JavaScript and SQLite to keep a working prototype inside the time and budget. The usability study is the one planned item with no work done on it yet.
